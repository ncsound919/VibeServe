# Claude (Anthropic) Design System
- Warm cream canvas (#faf9f5), warm dark ink (#141413)
- Coral primary (#cc785c) — warm, humanist, deliberately not blue
- Copernicus/Tiempos slab-serif display at weight 400, negative tracking
- StyreneB/Inter humanist sans body — literary editorial voice
- Three surface modes: cream canvas, light cream cards, dark navy mockups
- Coral only on primary CTAs and full-bleed coral callout cards
- Code editor mockups on dark navy surfaces as primary product showcase
- Serif headlines + cream canvas = editorial feels like a magazine, not SaaS
