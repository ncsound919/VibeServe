# Notion Design System
- Warm minimalism: cream canvas (#fffcf8), serif headings (Tiempos/Georgia), soft surfaces
- Black ink (#1a1a1a) for text, muted gray (#9b9b9b) for secondary
- Light gray borders (#e8e8e8), 3-6px minimal radius
- Weight 400-500 throughout, generous whitespace, editorial spacing
